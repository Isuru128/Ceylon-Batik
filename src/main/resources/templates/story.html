Born in the heart of Colombo

Founded in 1987 by a passionate family of batik artisans, Ceylon Batik began as a humble workshop in the vibrant streets of Colombo. Our vision was simple: to preserve the intricate beauty of traditional Sri Lankan batik while creating garments that resonate with modern elegance.

We honor the ancient wax-resist dyeing techniques that have graced our island since the Dutch colonial era. Every motif we design is deeply rooted in our heritage, drawing inspiration from lush tropical flora, ancient temple artistry, and the endless Indian Ocean.

Today, run by the third generation, Ceylon Batik stands as a testament to authenticity and enduring craft. We remain committed to sustainable practices, empowering local communities, and delivering unparalleled island fashion to the world.

"Every thread we weave carries the memory of the wax, the dye, and the hands that shaped it."
Our Technique

The Art of Wax & Dye

1
🖊️

Sketch & Design

Artists meticulously sketch intricate motifs inspired by Sri Lanka's vibrant flora, majestic temples, and ocean waves onto premium cotton and silk.

2
🕯️

Wax Resist

Hot liquid wax is delicately applied by hand using a traditional canting tool, creating a protective barrier against the dyes.

3
🎨

Natural Dye

The fabric is immersed in vivid natural dye baths. The process is repeated layer by layer to build complex, rich colour palettes.

4
✨

Finishing

The wax is carefully boiled away, revealing the final design. The fabric is then washed and sun-dried, ready to be tailored.

Meet the hands behind the craft

Our dedicated team of 12 master artisans in Colombo is the heartbeat of Ceylon Batik. Each member of our artisan family has spent decades perfecting their specific role in the batik lifecycle.

From the precise hand of the pattern designer to the intuition of our dye masters and the meticulous care of our finishing experts, it takes a harmonious community to bring each garment to life. We take pride in sustaining these traditional livelihoods and passing the torch to a new generation of creatives.
